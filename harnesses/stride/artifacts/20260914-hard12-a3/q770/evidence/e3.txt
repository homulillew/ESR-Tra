He is also the convenor of the Cambridge Centenary Symposium, a yearly gathering that examines neglected British authors on the centenary of their birth. In 2007 the writer will be Christopher Caudwell, following on from TH White (2006), Rex Warner (2005), Patrick Hamilton (2004) and Edward Upward (2003).
