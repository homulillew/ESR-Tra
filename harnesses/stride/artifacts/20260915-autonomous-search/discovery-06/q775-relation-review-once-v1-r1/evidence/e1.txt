Advisors

Program Director

Chigozie Obioma was born in Akure, Nigeria. His two novels, The Fishermen (2015) and An Orchestra of Minorities (2019) were shortlisted for The Booker Prize and have been translated into 30 languages. He has an LA Times book prize, the prestigious Internationalerpris, FT/Oppenheimer prize for fiction, an NAACP Image award and has been nominated for two dozen prizes for fiction. He was named one of Foreign Policy's 100 Leading Global Thinkers in 2015 and served as a judge of the Booker prize in 2021. He is the Helen S. Lanier Distinguished Professor of English and Creative Writing at the University of Georgia, and the program director of the Oxbelly Writers retreat. His third novel, The Road to the Country, was published in 2024.

Program Manager

Rosie is a production and event manager based in Athens, Greece, with extensive experience in coordinating high-profile film festivals and cultural events. She holds a degree in Communication, Media and Culture from Athens and pursued further studies in Art History in Paris, culminating in a Master's in Culture & Tourism from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne. Her professional experience includes collaborations with cultural organizations and festivals worldwide, including UNESCO in New York, the Rolex Art Festival, Les Rencontres d'Arles Photo Festival among others. Since 2018, she has served as Festival Coordinator and Special Events Manager for the French Film Festival of Greece, and since 2022, she has been the Head of Production for the Athens International Children's Film Festival. Driven by a deep passion for cultural engagement and sustainable tourism, she continually seeks innovative ways to connect communities through the arts. In her free time, she enjoys playing music and gardening.
